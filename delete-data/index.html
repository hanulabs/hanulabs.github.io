Delete Your Hanu Data

Hanu (Korean learning app) by Hanu Labs

Hanu has no user accounts. Most of your data lives only on your phone, and the rest is held by the services listed in our Privacy Policy. Here is how to delete it.

Step 1: Delete the data on your phone

Your lessons, progress, review schedule and settings are stored only on your device. To delete them, uninstall Hanu, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Hanu → Storage → Clear data. This takes effect immediately.

Step 2: Ask us to delete the rest

  1. Send an email to hanu.support@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Hanu data”.
  2. If you have ever subscribed to Hanu Plus, include the Google Play order number (it starts with “GPA.” and is in your Google Play purchase receipt email) so we can find your records.
  3. We will confirm by email and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your subscription customer record at RevenueCat (anonymous user ID and purchase history).
  • Crash reports linked to your installation, where we can identify them.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics) are not linked to your name or email, so we usually cannot single them out. They are deleted automatically after 14 months.
  • Crash reports are deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Payment and tax records of past purchases are kept by Google Play, and by us where the law requires it (typically up to 10 years), for accounting purposes only.
  • Deleting your data does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
